01 / STARTING POINT

The work started from a blank communication foundation.

IMWORKS operates across strategy, creativity, production and execution, with capabilities spanning events, experience, exhibition, interior and fabrication, garment and merchandise, creative and advertising.

NUSAKARA began by researching the business and understanding how those capabilities could be presented as one coherent story rather than a list of separate services.

04 / COMPANY PROFILE

The company profile became the first expression of the system.

The narrative was structured as a journey: who IMWORKS is, the challenge it solves, what it does, its capability pillars, production, process, selected work and client value.

The aim was not simply to make a presentation look polished. It was to make a complex business easier to understand, present and remember.
