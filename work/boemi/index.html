01 / STARTING POINT

Capital was available. The business direction was not.

The investor initially had capital but did not yet have a clear business to deploy it into. NUSAKARA was brought in for consultation: where should the capital go, what business made sense, and how could it be built into an operating venture?

Rather than treating the engagement as a conventional creative assignment, NUSAKARA took responsibility for developing the business opportunity from the ground up.

Find the opportunity. Then build the system around it.Business direction
02 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Used smartphones became the business opportunity.

NUSAKARA proposed a used-smartphone business model built around the practical realities of buying, checking, pricing, stocking and reselling devices.

The challenge was larger than identifying a product category. The business needed an operating model: how units enter the business, how they are recorded, how sales are handled, how COD works, how money moves, how roles are assigned and how the owner can see the business clearly.

01 / CONCEPT

Define the venture

Turn an open investment question into a concrete used-smartphone business direction.

02 / MODEL

Define the flow

Structure the operational journey from inventory intake to sales, payment, COD and reporting.

03 / FROM IDEA TO OPERATION

The investor approved the direction. NUSAKARA was asked to build it.

After agreeing on the opportunity, the investor delegated the end-to-end development of BOEMI to NUSAKARA — taking the project from zero toward an operating business.

This expanded the role beyond strategy and creative production into business structuring, operational design, supply-chain access, product development and ongoing system refinement.

04 / BUILDING THE SUPPLY CHAIN

Access matters as much as the idea.

NUSAKARA opened and developed access to distributor channels so BOEMI could move from a business concept toward an actual supply path for used smartphones.

The supply side became part of the venture architecture: a business cannot be operated only through a digital interface if the physical inventory and sourcing process are undefined.

05 / THE OPERATING MODEL

BOEMI was designed as an operating system for the business.

The digital product was built around the workflows the business actually needs rather than treated as a standalone app.

01 / InventoryDevice intake, specifications, condition, IMEI, purchase cost, asking price, photos and stock status.
02 / Sales & CustomersOrders, customer records, seller attribution, transaction information and sales status.
03 / CODFulfillment, assigned COD staff, handover, payment recording and proof-of-handover workflow.
04 / FinancePayments, expenses, estimated margin, reporting and operational financial visibility.
05 / Roles & AccessRole-aware views and permissions across Owner, Sales, COD, Cashier and Inventory responsibilities.
06 / ReportingBusiness reporting and allocation logic designed to give the owner a clearer operational picture.
