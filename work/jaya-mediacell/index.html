01 / CONTEXT

The business already existed. Its digital presence needed a foundation.

Jaya Mediacell provides repair services for Android devices, iPhone, MacBook, laptops, and computers. At an early stage, the challenge was not simply to produce posts, but to make the business easier to discover, understand, and remember.

NUSAKARA was engaged for digital marketing and social media management, with an initial focus on digital presence, service communication, content systems, and organic distribution.

02 / THE PROBLEM

Awareness does not automatically create a reason to trust.

01 / PRESENCE

No audience foundation

Instagram was at 7 followers and TikTok at 0 when NUSAKARA began managing the channels.

02 / CLARITY

Services needed translation

A broad repair-service offering needed to become simple, relevant communication that potential customers could understand.

03 / CONSISTENCY

Content needed to become a system

Activity needed to move from isolated posts toward a repeatable system of content, formats, distribution, and learning.

04 / JOURNEY

Attention needs a next step

Attention from social media needed a clearer path toward business action: asking, contacting, or visiting.

08 / NUSAKARA'S ROLE

NUSAKARA’s role should be clear.

NUSAKARA was responsible for digital marketing and social media management: direction, content planning, creative execution, publishing/distribution, and digital performance reading.

StrategyDigital and content direction based on business stage and communication needs.
CreativeConcept, copy, visual content, Reels, photo posts, carousels, and creative execution.
SocialInstagram, TikTok, Facebook, publishing, monitoring, and initial response within managed channels.
ReportingPerformance reading and evaluation to inform the next development cycle.

What this case does not claim

WhatsApp customer service, staff response speed, store operations, additional repair-process costs, and operational issues after a lead moves to the business side are not presented as outcomes of NUSAKARA’s social media work. These factors should be evaluated separately so attribution remains fair.

09 / WHAT WE LEARNED

Growth is not only about adding numbers.

The first phase shows that building digital presence from a very small baseline requires more than posting frequency. Positioning, content systems, distribution, and the customer journey need to move together.

The next stage should not be limited to follower growth. It should strengthen audience quality, intent, lead handling, conversion tracking, Google Business presence, and the link between digital activity and recorded transactions.
