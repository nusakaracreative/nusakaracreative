CASE STUDY / 001

JAYA
MEDIACELL

Building a stronger digital presence for a growing device service business through social media strategy, content and consistent digital execution.

DISCIPLINEDigital Marketing
SCOPESocial Media & Content
PERIOD15 Aug — 13 Sep 2026
PARTNERNUSAKARA CREATIVE
01 / THE CONTEXT

A new business needed to become visible.

Jaya Mediacell operates in device repair, serving Android, iPhone, MacBook, laptop and computer customers.

THE STARTING POINT

From almost no digital audience.

At the beginning of the engagement, Instagram had 7 followers and TikTok had 0 followers according to the project data.

THE OBJECTIVE

Build awareness first.

The first phase focused on making people understand what Jaya Mediacell is, what it repairs, where it operates and why the business should be considered when a device needs service.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Attention is not the same as clarity.

A new service business has to establish its presence while competing for attention with more established names.

01 — Establish presence

Create a consistent social presence that communicates the business clearly across channels.

02 — Create relevance

Turn repair services, problems and solutions into content that audiences can understand and engage with.

03 / THE APPROACH

Make the business easier to understand.

The work connected content planning, platform execution and a clear communication direction.

01

Position

Introduce Jaya Mediacell as a device service destination rather than simply another repair account.

02

Content

Build a mix of educational, promotional and service-oriented content for social discovery.

03

Distribution

Use Instagram and TikTok as the primary organic channels for awareness and audience growth.

04

Learn

Use platform performance and audience signals to identify what should be developed next.

04 / EXECUTION

Strategy became a consistent stream of work.

The engagement translated the direction into recurring content and social media activity.

01 / CONTENT SYSTEM

28 Instagram Posts

13 Reels, 10 photo posts and 5 carousels during the reported period — a recurring content stream rather than isolated posts.

02 / PLATFORM

Instagram + TikTok

Organic social activity focused on building discovery and audience momentum.

03 / COMMUNICATION

Awareness → Action

Content was designed to move audiences from knowing the business toward contacting or visiting it.
