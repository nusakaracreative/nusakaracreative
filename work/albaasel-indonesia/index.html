01 / THE CONTEXT

The problem was bigger than social media.

When NUSAKARA began working with Albaasel, the organization was going through a difficult transition. A significant part of the existing management team had resigned, and the organization was exposed to dependencies between players, parents, management and the previous coaching figure.

The owner was even considering whether the organization could continue. NUSAKARA's initial brief was to help find a solution and handle social media, but field observation revealed a broader organizational problem.

Albaasel did not only need better communication. It needed a stronger system.Initial diagnosis
02 / THE DIAGNOSIS

The visible problem was not the real problem.

01 / MANAGEMENT

Management dependency

Management positions were closely connected to the parents of existing players, creating a dependency between player retention and management capacity.

02 / COACHING

Coach dependency

Personal relationships between players, parents and the previous coach created a risk that people would leave together.

03 / SYSTEM

Weak enforcement

Albaasel already had SOPs, but consistency and consequences for non-compliance were not strong enough to create organizational discipline.

04 / INFORMATION

Information dependency

Important records were maintained by previous management, exposing the organization when people left without complete handover.

05 / ALIGNMENT

Communication gap

The issue was not always a lack of communication. It was the distance between different sides of the organization and the absence of alignment.

03 / THE RESET

Rebuilding Albaasel from the inside.

04 / FROM SURVIVAL TO STABILITY

The transformation was visible beyond social media.

The source material describes increasingly lively stands, more children joining Albaasel, and more friendly matches with other football schools. Those activities also became meaningful marketing assets while creating additional opportunities for players to develop their skills and gain competitive experience.

The organization was beginning to move again. NUSAKARA's stated objective was not simply a busier social account, but an organization capable of operating normally, consistently and sustainably without the owner having to personally step into every operational matter.

The system is the measure—not the account.Operating principle
05 / EXPANSION & VALIDATION

From insight to Albaasel Cimahi.

As Albaasel regained stability, NUSAKARA identified a potential opportunity in Cimahi through tournaments, direct interaction with parents, and field research. The new location is being developed from the ground up rather than treated as a simple marketing campaign.

The work currently spans management and operations, system design, SOP, finance, home-base selection, permits, recruitment, digital marketing, social media and content for the new location.

08 / NUSAKARA'S ROLE

The scope expanded because the responsibility expanded.

The initial mandate was to help find a solution and handle Albaasel's social media. The relationship evolved from digital marketing and social media management into content production, strategic advisory, management and business expansion.

03 / CONTENT PRODUCTIONCreating structured content that supports communication and marketing.
04 / STRATEGIC ADVISORYHelping define direction, priorities and the underlying organizational system.
05 / MANAGEMENTWorking alongside Albaasel on management and operating structure.
06 / BUSINESS EXPANSIONDeveloping Albaasel Cimahi from the ground up, including operations, system, SOP, finance, location, permits, recruitment and launch preparation.
09 / THE PARTNERSHIP

Not replacing the owner. Building independence.

NUSAKARA does not see its role as taking over Albaasel's identity or replacing its owner. The role is to work alongside the organization, build the structure it needs, and help create a system that can eventually stand on its own.

Independence through a better system.Partnership principle
