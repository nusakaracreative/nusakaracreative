015 / TYPOGRAPHY

TYPE WITH
STRUCTURE.

The official type system is Manrope, with Inter as fallback when Manrope is unavailable. Typography should feel large, confident, clean, editorial and structured.

PRIMARY / HEADLINES

Manrope

ExtraBold / Bold for headlines; SemiBold for subheads and emphasis; Medium / Regular for body, navigation and supporting information.

SECONDARY / BODY & UI

Inter

Fallback typeface when Manrope is unavailable.

Principle: strong hierarchy, controlled spacing, high contrast and minimal decoration. Typography should feel editorial rather than ornamental.

016 / TYPOGRAPHIC PRINCIPLES

CLARITY
OVER NOISE.

01

Editorial hierarchy

Headlines carry presence. Supporting information stays quiet enough to preserve the reading order.

02

Controlled spacing

Whitespace is an active part of the identity. Do not fill every available space.

03

Consistent rhythm

Section spacing, alignment and type scale should feel deliberate across pages and formats.

04

Consistency over novelty

Do not change typefaces simply to make an individual asset feel different.

017 / PHOTOGRAPHY

REAL WORK.
REAL ENERGY.

NUSAKARA imagery should feel cinematic, intentional and human. Photography is not decoration; it is part of the communication system.

01 / CHARACTER

Cinematic

Strong light, depth, atmosphere and considered framing.

02 / CHARACTER

Authentic

Real people, real environments and believable production moments.

03 / CHARACTER

Editorial

Clean composition, intentional crops and visual restraint.

018 / GRAPHIC LANGUAGE

THE SYSTEM
BEHIND THE LOOK.

Layouts should use editorial structure, strong alignment, controlled grids and purposeful accents. The system should support the work, not compete with it.

GRID.

Use clear columns, consistent margins and deliberate asymmetry only when it improves hierarchy.

ACCENT.

Orange and red are signals, not backgrounds for everything. Use them to direct attention and create movement.

019 / DIGITAL & MOTION

CONTROLLED
MOTION.

Motion should feel cinematic, intentional and restrained. Transitions, typography and logo movement should support the story rather than become the story.

Motion

Purposeful movement, clean transitions and readable pacing.

Video

Human, cinematic, production-led and visually considered.

AI

AI may assist visual exploration and production, but it must not alter locked brand assets.

021 / AI BRAND RULES

THE LOGO
STAYS HUMAN.

NUSAKARA uses AI as a creative and production tool — never as an authority over the brand identity.
